ABOUT US

Welcome to Boogie Town - Where the '70s Come Alive!

Step into BTB (Boogie Town Burger) and take a groovy trip back to the golden era of the 1970s. More than just a burger joint, we're a time machine that transports you to the heart of retro America, right here in Qatar.

Born from a passion for the golden age of diners and the soulful vibes of the '70s, Boogie Town Burger brings authentic retro charm to the modern Qatar dining scene. We believe that great food should come with great atmosphere.

At BTB, we're not just serving burgers; we're serving memories. From our signature retro burgers to our vintage fries, each bite is designed to transport you back to simpler times.
